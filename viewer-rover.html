Screws from the kit

All M4 parts come from the Mywish kit and the HANGLIFE inserts. The kit's per-length counts aren't listed anywhere, so count yours against the screw rows in the table.

Check in the tote test

  • The motors hang 25.7 mm (1.01 in) above the bottom of the flights. If the rover sinks deeper than that, they plow corn.
  • Rotating hub to motor screw heads: 1.65 mm. Set the 5 mm gap between plate and cap before you tighten the hub.
  • The shield rests on the Uno's USB jack. Put a strip of electrical tape on top of the jack first.
  • Every sleeve screw head is at least 11 mm from the flight, so it never sits in the flight's path.
