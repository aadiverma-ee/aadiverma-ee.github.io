Electroluminescence sphere fixture

Aadi Verma, summer 2026. A solar cell on a light-bulb base, built to be measured inside an integrating sphere.

    Measured inside the sphere

    The fixture screws into the lamp socket like a bulb. In forward bias the cell emits light, the white wall scatters it until the whole sphere is evenly lit, and the fibre port passes a sample to the spectrometer. A baffle keeps the port from seeing the cell directly.
